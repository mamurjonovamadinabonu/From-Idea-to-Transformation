Section 4 • Transformation Pathway

🗺️ The 6-Step Pathway from Idea to Business Value

1. Problem Definition

Define clear business problem and customer jobs-to-be-done.

2. Ideation & AI Scope

Formulate value proposition and select appropriate AI approach.

3. MVP Experimentation

Build rapid prototype to test critical value & feasibility hypotheses.

4. Evidence Collection

Measure real pilot metrics (speed, cost, CSAT, error rate).

5. Business Scaling

Integrate verified AI workflow into core organizational operations.

6. Governance & Review

Maintain continuous ethical audit, model updates, and human oversight.

Section 5 • Leadership & Skills (7 min)

Innovation Leadership Under Uncertainty

🛡️ Building Psychological Safety

🧭 Alignment & Strategy

Connecting innovative project experiments directly to corporate strategic priorities.
